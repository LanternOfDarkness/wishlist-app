## 2024-05-24 - Missing form associations and aria-labels on icon buttons
**Learning:** Found a pattern of missing `htmlFor` to `id` mapping in complex form component groups (e.g. `wishlist-filters`), and missing `aria-label`s on icon-only interactive elements (e.g. avatar triggers and copy link buttons).
**Action:** When adding new form elements or interactive UI, proactively ensure form labels have `htmlFor` attributes pointing to correctly IDs, and all icon buttons have an explicit `aria-label` or screen reader accessible text.
## 2025-02-12 - Interactive Checkboxes Need Pointer Feedback
**Learning:** Native `type="checkbox"` elements and their corresponding `<label>` wrappers lack interactive visual feedback (like a pointer cursor) out of the box. Simply applying `disabled:cursor-not-allowed` to `<Label>` components directly from Shadcn or native labels does not work intuitively because the `:disabled` pseudo-class typically targets the form inputs, not the labels themselves (though Tailwind handles `disabled:` on some elements differently, the `cursor-pointer` must be on the label itself to show intent).
**Action:** Always apply `cursor-pointer disabled:cursor-not-allowed` directly to the `input[type="checkbox"]` and `cursor-pointer disabled:cursor-not-allowed` to the wrapping `<label>` to override default browser behavior and provide better interactive affordances for users.
